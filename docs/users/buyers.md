# 🛒 Buyers - Marketplace & Trading Platform

## 🎯 Target Audience

Buyers represent the demand side of the agricultural marketplace, connecting with farmers and suppliers to source agricultural products. This role is designed for:

- **Agricultural Traders**: Local and regional product distributors
- **Food Processors**: Companies that transform raw agricultural products
- **Exporters**: International trade companies and shipping agents
- **Retailers**: Supermarkets, grocery stores, and food markets
- **Restaurants & Hotels**: Food service industry buyers
- **Manufacturers**: Companies using agricultural products as raw materials
- **Government Agencies**: Food security and procurement organizations
- **NGOs**: Humanitarian aid and development organizations

## 🚀 Getting Started for Buyers

### 1. Business Registration
```markdown
1. Complete business profile and verification
2. Provide business registration and tax documents
3. Set up payment methods and banking information
4. Configure shipping and delivery preferences
5. Set quality standards and certification requirements
6. Complete marketplace orientation and training
```

### 2. Essential Buyer Setup
```markdown
1. **Business Profile**: Complete company information and verification
2. **Payment Setup**: Configure payment methods and banking details
3. **Shipping Preferences**: Set delivery locations and timing
4. **Quality Standards**: Define product quality requirements
5. **Supplier Preferences**: Set preferred regions and certifications
6. **Export Setup**: Configure international trade requirements
```

### 3. Daily Buyer Workflow
```markdown
Morning (9-10 AM):
- Check market updates and price changes
- Review new product listings and supplier offers
- Analyze market trends and opportunities
- Check order status and delivery updates

Midday (2-3 PM):
- Negotiate with suppliers and place orders
- Review quality reports and product samples
- Update supplier relationships and ratings
- Process payments and manage invoices

Evening (5-6 PM):
- Review daily purchases and spending
- Plan next day's sourcing strategy
- Engage with supplier community
- Update market intelligence reports
```
